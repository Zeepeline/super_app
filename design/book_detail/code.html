Synopsis

In the silence of the obsidian fields, the rhythm of the old world still beats. "Obsidian Rhythm" explores the intersection of ancient geology and futuristic sonic landscapes, following a curator who uncovers a digital signal frozen in volcanic glass for over three millennia.

As the protagonist delves deeper into the rhythmic patterns, the boundaries between physical reality and digital simulation begin to blur. Vance crafts a visceral narrative that challenges our perception of time, memory, and the permanence of data in a shifting universe.

Praised for its "haunting atmosphere and architectural prose," this volume stands as a cornerstone of modern speculative editorial fiction.
