# Ultimate Productivity Knowledge Base & Task Templates

This document is a comprehensive resource for AI-driven task recommendations. It integrates global productivity frameworks (GTD, Eisenhower, 1-3-5 Rule) with Hourly Context Mapping and Energy Management.

---

## 1. System Logic: How AI Should Recommend Tasks
AI MUST follow these priority steps when generating a recommendation:
1.  **Check Time**: Map current `DateTime` to the Hourly Blocks in Section 2.
2.  **Check Profile**: Identify if the user is in "Professional" or "Student" mode (default to Professional if unknown).
3.  **Check Energy**: Suggest "Deep Work" for high-energy windows and "Admin/Routine" for low-energy windows.
4.  **Apply 1-3-5 Rule**: Ensure the user doesn't have more than 1 Big, 3 Medium, and 5 Small tasks active.

## 2. Hourly Context & Energy Mapping (The Daily Engine)

### [05:00 - 08:00] Early Morning: "The Foundation"
- **Energy**: Rising / High Focus.
- **Goal**: Health, Planning, and Zero Distraction.
- **Templates**:
    - **Morning Hydration**: Drink 500ml of water. (Small)
    - **Daily Review**: Look at calendar and pick the "Big 1" for today. (Medium)
    - **Physical Mobility**: 10m stretching or yoga. (Small)
    - **Intellectual Fuel**: Read 15m of a non-fiction book. (Medium)
    - **Meditation**: 5-10m of mindful breathing. (Small)

### [08:00 - 12:00] Morning: "Biological Prime Time"
- **Energy**: Peak Cognitive Capacity.
- **Goal**: Deep Work. Hardest tasks first.
- **Templates**:
    - **The "Big 1" Sprint**: 90-120m block for your most important project. (Big)
    - **Technical Deep Dive**: Bug hunting, complex coding, or research. (Big)
    - **Strategic Drafting**: Writing proposals, docs, or thesis chapters. (Big)
    - **Intensive Study**: Solving complex problem sets or revision. (Big)

### [12:00 - 14:00] Mid-Day: "The Reset"
- **Energy**: Post-lunch Dip.
- **Goal**: Nutrition and Cognitive Cooling.
- **Templates**:
    - **Power Nap**: 20m restorative rest. (Small)
    - **Mindful Eating**: Lunch away from desk. (Small)
    - **Walking Brainstorm**: 15m walk to refresh the brain. (Small)
    - **Digital Detox**: Close all tabs and put phone away for 30m. (Small)

### [14:00 - 17:00] Afternoon: "The Execution Block"
- **Energy**: Medium / Stable.
- **Goal**: Collaboration, Admin, and Shallow Work.
- **Templates**:
    - **Inbox Zero Batch**: Process all emails/Slack in one block. (Medium)
    - **Administrative Sync**: Meetings, calls, and follow-ups. (Medium)
    - **Quick Win List**: Knock out 5 small tasks (less than 5m each). (Small)
    - **File Organization**: Sort downloads, rename assets, sync cloud. (Small)
    - **Progress Update**: Log time/expenses or update project boards. (Small)

### [17:00 - 19:00] Evening: "The Transition"
- **Energy**: Winding Down.
- **Goal**: Closing the loop and physical reset.
- **Templates**:
    - **Shutdown Ritual**: Clear the physical/digital desk. Set the Big 1 for tomorrow. (Medium)
    - **High-Intensity Exercise**: Gym, run, or intensive home workout. (Big)
    - **Logistics**: Grocery run or household essentials shopping. (Medium)
    - **Workspace Prep**: Prep coffee, clothes, and tools for tomorrow. (Small)

### [19:00 - 22:00] Night: "Leisure & Reflection"
- **Energy**: Creative Spike / Low Logic.
- **Goal**: Hobbies, connection, and restorative habits.
- **Templates**:
    - **Creative Hobby**: Working on art, musicplayer, or side projects. (Big)
    - **Social Connection**: Call/visit family or friends. (Medium)
    - **Daily Win Audit**: Write down 3 things that went well today. (Small)
    - **Light Reading**: Fiction or light articles before bed. (Small)
    - **Self-Care**: Nightly skincare or relaxing bath. (Small)

## 4. Priority Frameworks (Grounding Rules)

### The 1-3-5 Rule
- **1 Big Task**: Takes 3-4 hours. Essential.
- **3 Medium Tasks**: Take 1-2 hours each. Important.
- **5 Small Tasks**: Take 15-30 minutes. Quick wins.

### Eisenhower Matrix
- **Important & Urgent**: Do Immediately.
- **Important & Not Urgent**: Schedule (AI should focus on suggesting these to prevent stress).
- **Urgent & Not Important**: Minimize/Delegate.
- **Not Important & Not Urgent**: Eliminate.

---

## 5. Habit Stacking (Atomic Habits)
Use these to help the user "latch" new tasks onto old routines:
- **Stack 1**: "After I finish my morning coffee, I will write down my Big 1 task."
- **Stack 2**: "Before I close my laptop, I will clear my physical desk."
- **Stack 3**: "After I eat lunch, I will walk for 10 minutes without my phone."
- **Stack 4**: "Before I go to sleep, I will set my alarm and prep my workout clothes."

---

## 6. Energy Management Hacks
- **The 2-Minute Rule**: If a suggested task takes < 2 mins, AI should prompt: "Do this now, it's quick!"
- **The 20-20-20 Rule**: Every 20 mins of screen time, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
- **Pomodoro**: Use for "Shallow Work" (25m work / 5m break).
- **Deep Blocks**: Use for "Deep Work" (90m work / 15m break).
