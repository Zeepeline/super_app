## 2024-05-24 - [Tactile & Semantic Feedback in Music Apps]
**Learning:** In high-interaction mobile apps like music players, tactile feedback (Haptics) and clear semantic labels significantly enhance the perceived quality and accessibility of the interface. Users rely on haptics for "blind" interactions with playback controls.
**Action:** Always include tooltips (for semantics) and appropriate haptic feedback (light for toggles, medium for primary actions) on icon-only buttons.
